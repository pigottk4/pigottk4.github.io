🔧 How It Works

Think of finops-cost-management-azure as your smart financial assistant for the cloud. Here's the simple breakdown:

  • Connects to your Azure accounts: It securely links to your Azure subscription(s) to see your spending.
  • Sets your budget limits: You tell it how much you want to spend each month.
  • Monitors in real-time: It continuously tracks your spending and compares it to your budget.
  • Alerts you early: If you're approaching your limit, you get notified so you can take action.
  • Enforces policies: Automatically applies rules to prevent unnecessary spending.
